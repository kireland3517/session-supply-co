# Session Supply Co. — Design Brainstorm

## Chosen Approach: Editorial Calm

**Design Movement:** Modern Editorial Wellness — think Kinfolk magazine meets a structured digital toolkit.

**Core Principles:**
1. Whitespace as a primary design element — sections breathe, nothing crowds
2. Serif headlines (Cormorant Garamond) anchor the editorial feel; Inter body text keeps it readable and modern
3. Color used sparingly — deep forest green as the anchor, sage and slate as supporting tones, clay as a single warm accent
4. Content hierarchy is everything — users are time-constrained, so visual scanning must be effortless

**Color Philosophy:**
- Deep Forest Green `#1E4D40` — authority, calm, trust (primary brand color)
- Cool Sage `#8FB8A8` — supporting, botanical, soft
- Dusty Slate Blue `#7A9BB0` — modern, editorial, cool contrast
- Muted Clay `#B5735A` — warm accent, used only for CTAs and highlights
- Linen `#F7F4EF` — warm white for section backgrounds
- Near-Black `#2C2C2C` — body text, maximum legibility

**Layout Paradigm:**
- Asymmetric editorial grids — hero sections with left-aligned text and right-side imagery
- Section-based page structure with generous vertical rhythm
- Product cards in clean 3-column grids on desktop, 1-column on mobile
- No full-width image banners — prefer contained, framed imagery

**Signature Elements:**
1. Thin botanical line-art SVG dividers and icons (leaf, sprig, circle)
2. Oversized serif display numbers for statistics/social proof
3. Subtle green left-border accents on blockquotes and feature callouts

**Interaction Philosophy:**
- Hover states are gentle — slight elevation (shadow), no aggressive color shifts
- CTAs are confident but not loud — forest green fill, no gradients
- Navigation is minimal — sticky top bar, clean links, no mega-menus

**Animation:**
- Fade-in-up on scroll for section content (subtle, 300ms)
- Hover: product cards lift with a soft shadow transition
- No parallax, no heavy motion — respects the calm, focused audience

**Typography System:**
- Display: Cormorant Garamond — used for H1, H2, pull quotes
- Body: Inter — used for H3, body text, labels, nav
- Scale: 14px base, 1.6 line-height for body; display sizes 48–72px on desktop
